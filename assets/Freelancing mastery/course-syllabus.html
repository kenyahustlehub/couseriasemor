Freelancing Mastery — Course Syllabus

This syllabus maps the Freelancing Mastery course path, covering mindset, client work, pricing, and growth strategies.

  • Lesson 1: Course Outline — watch the course introduction to learn the program structure, key goals, and how to start building a successful freelance career. Watch Course Outline
  • Lesson 2: Upwork Introduction & Fees — learn how Upwork works, understand platform fees, and prepare your pricing strategy for success.
  • Lesson 3: Signing Up for Upwork — follow the step-by-step process for creating your Upwork profile, setting up your skills, and launching your first freelancing presence.
  • Lesson 4: Understanding Upwork Profile Settings — learn how to optimize your Upwork profile settings, privacy options, and visibility to attract the right clients.
  • Lesson 5: Building Highly Targeted Specialized Profiles — discover how to build specialized Upwork profiles that attract premium clients and improve your hiring opportunities.
  • Lesson 6: Build Your Upwork Profile Case Studies — practice building profile case studies with real examples so your Upwork profile clearly highlights value and wins client trust.
  • Lesson 7: Deals Module Introduction — get introduced to the Deals module, including managing proposals, client negotiations, and project agreements on Upwork.
  • Lesson 8: Profitable Freelance Sales Process — learn the profitable freelance sales process for closing high-value deals and maximizing your earnings on Upwork.
  • Lesson 9: Finding Your First Clients — learn where to find freelance clients, how to pitch, and how to win your first projects.
  • Lesson 10: Project Management for Freelancers — manage deliverables, communication, and timelines like a pro.
  • Lesson 11: Building a Portfolio — create a compelling portfolio that showcases your skills and attracts high-quality clients.
  • Lesson 12: Brand Positioning and Specialization — define your niche and position yourself to stand out in competitive freelance marketplaces.
  • Lesson 13: Scaling Your Freelance Business — learn systems, repeatable workflows, and upsell tactics to grow your income.

Course outcomes

  • Know how to begin freelancing with confidence and purpose.
  • Understand how to find clients, set pricing, and write winning proposals.
  • Learn how to manage freelance projects professionally and deliver value.
  • Gain a clear path to scaling your freelance business and delivering consistent results.
