AI Image Generation Tips

Best practices for creating effective visuals with AI, especially for Midjourney-style workflows.

  • Choose a clear subject and a strong visual concept.
  • Use mood words like dramatic, minimal, energetic, or cinematic.
  • Control perspective with terms like close-up, aerial, or isometric.
  • Limit the prompt to the most important visual details.
  • Experiment with color palettes and lighting descriptions.
