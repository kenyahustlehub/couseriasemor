AI Tools Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare your AI workflows and deliverables for marketing, business, and productivity.

  1. Define the target audience and business goal.
  2. Choose the best AI tools for writing, image creation, and automation.
  3. Gather existing content, prompts, and brand voice examples.
  4. Set output format: email, blog, social post, contract, or image.
  5. Review outputs for accuracy, ethics, and tone.
  6. Refine prompts and run a second edit pass.
  7. Organize final assets in folders for reuse.
