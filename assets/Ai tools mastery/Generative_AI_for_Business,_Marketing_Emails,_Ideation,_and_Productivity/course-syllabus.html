AI Tools Mastery — Course Syllabus

This syllabus guides students through the practical AI course path for business, marketing emails, ideation, and productivity.

  • Lesson 1: Write a Contract for Business — legal AI planning and practical templates.
  • Lesson 2: Understanding AI Fundamentals — AI concepts, tool categories, and strategy.
  • Lesson 3: Getting Started with ChatGPT — prompt engineering, templates, and best practices.
  • Lesson 4: Creating Content with AI — writing frameworks, editing workflows, and templates.
  • Lesson 5: AI Image Generation with Midjourney — visual prompts, composition, and creative production.
  • Lesson 6: Combining Multiple AI Tools — workflows for productivity and creative integration.
  • Lesson 7: AI Voice Generation with Murf.ai — creating professional audio for presentations, videos, and marketing.
  • Lesson 8: AI Voice Generation with WellSaid Labs — turn scripts into natural-sounding voiceovers for videos and ads.
  • Lesson 9: Clone Your Voice with ElevenLabs — build custom voice branding and AI narration from your own speech.
  • Lesson 10: Text-to-Voice with Play.ht — convert written scripts into realistic voiceovers for podcasts and videos.
  • Lesson 11: Fix Audio with Free AI Filters — clean and polish voice recordings using free AI audio tools.

Course outcomes

  • Build AI prompts that deliver real business value.
  • Create high-converting marketing copy using ChatGPT.
  • Generate legal, sales, and productivity materials quickly.
  • Use image AI to build visual assets for campaigns and ideas.
