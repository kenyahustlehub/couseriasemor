AI Fundamentals Cheat Sheet

A quick-reference set of terms, tool types, and best practices for AI-powered business work.

Common AI Types

  • Text generation: chatbots, copywriting, content outline creation.
  • Image generation: visuals, social media graphics, product concepts.
  • Automation: process workflows, email sequencing, task triggers.

Prompt structure

  1. Goal: What should the output achieve?
  2. Context: Who is the audience and what is the tone?
  3. Format: specify the exact type of result needed.
  4. Examples: give one or two sample outputs.

Review checklist

  • Is the output useful for the job?
  • Does it follow brand voice and structure?
  • Did you fact-check and remove errors?
