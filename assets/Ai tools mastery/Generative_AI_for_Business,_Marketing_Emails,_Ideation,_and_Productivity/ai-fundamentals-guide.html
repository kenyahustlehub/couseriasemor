AI Fundamentals Guide

This guide explains the core AI concepts needed to use tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney, and automation platforms.

Key concepts

  • AI models: understanding how GPT, image models, and automation logic work.
  • Prompting: how to ask clear questions and set context.
  • Workflow: linking drafting, review, and publishing steps.
  • Ethics: factual accuracy, bias review, and safe use.

Best practices

  • Start with a clear goal and example outputs.
  • Give the AI enough context without overloading it.
  • Use step-by-step prompts for complex work.
  • Always verify AI answers before publishing.
